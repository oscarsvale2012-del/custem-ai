What VYRON is

VYRON is a desktop app for making Roblox games with Claude. This page explains what data your VYRON account holds and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, and your password stored as a secure hash by our account provider (Supabase). If you use “Continue with Google”, we receive your email address and basic profile (name, picture) from Google — nothing else.
  • Your VYRON data: settings, Roblox project names and ids, your own game ideas, picked ideas and your Claude chat history in VYRON, so they come back when you sign in again or on another PC.

What we don't store

  • Your Roblox sign-in, Roblox API keys and your Claude sign-in stay on your own PC and are never uploaded.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone except the services that run VYRON accounts (Supabase for accounts and storage, and Google if you choose to sign in with Google).

Who can see your data

Only you. Each account can only read and change its own data.
